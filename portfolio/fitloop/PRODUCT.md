# FitLoop — Product and experience design

## Design goal

Make local clothes alterations feel as easy to arrange as ordering a service online. The demo was influenced by the discoverability and approachable presentation of consumer marketplaces like Vinted and Depop, but focused on tailoring.

## Customer journey demonstrated

1. **Discover:** browse tailor profiles and narrow by service, area or other demo filters.
2. **Describe:** explain what needs adjusting through a guided alteration request, with sample image/option selection.
3. **Compare:** review demonstration quotes and choose an option.
4. **Book and track:** follow a simulated booking through progress states.
5. **Communicate:** explore a local messaging demonstration and customer/tailor dashboards.

The pitch also explored possible retail partnerships for fit-related returns. These were **product concepts**, not established partnerships.

## Tools and implementation scope

The existing [technical report](https://github.com/ahmedmadkour-dev/fitloop/blob/main/PROJECT_REPORT.md) records TypeScript, React, Vite/vinext conventions, Tailwind CSS and Cloudflare tooling. I directed the product requirements, visual presentation, iterations and website deployment, with code-generation assistance on substantial implementation work.

This was a prototype with demonstration data; there is no verified real multi-user marketplace backend, payment processing or live message delivery.

## Next steps, if revived

Interview local tailors, establish real service/pricing categories, test demand with a small pilot, define trust and complaints processes, then build authenticated data flows only after validating the business case.
