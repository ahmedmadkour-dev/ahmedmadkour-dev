## The problem

Clothing often gets returned or left unworn because it doesn't fit quite right. FitLoop was a team concept for connecting customers with local tailors, making alterations easier to discover, request and track instead of returning clothing.

## What I contributed

- Took responsibility for **product design and building the interactive website prototype**, including its appearance, customer journey and feature iterations.
- Bought the domain and deployed the demo for the pitch.
- Designed and developed the final pitch presentation with the team; explained the product demo, customer-acquisition approach and risks.
- Presented to judges and answered questions about the idea and business model.
- Used feedback to refine the prototype and pitch.

This was a **team entrepreneurship entry**. I describe my own responsibilities separately from the team's overall idea.

## What the interactive demo demonstrated

The prototype included a responsive marketplace, tailor discovery, search/filter controls, saved tailors, a multi-step alteration request, sample quote comparisons, booking progress, customer/tailor dashboards, messaging demonstrations and a retailer alteration journey.

It was **not** a live commercial marketplace. Users, quotes, payments and conversations in the demonstration were simulated; the demo did not include production multi-user authentication, real payments or a live shared marketplace database.

## Competition result

FitLoop was presented at the **Generating Genius Entrepreneurship Genius** business competition on **5 September 2026**. Our team did **not** win the overall competition. I received an **Outstanding Individual award**, with positive feedback on my contribution to the website and presentation.

The pitch was valuable because it exposed product and business-model questions we hadn't fully validated. In particular, our proposed valuation was insufficiently evidenced, and I learnt the difference between a convincing prototype and a defensible commercial case.

Read the [full competition account](COMPETITION.md).
